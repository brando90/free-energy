## Reading

(1) Hop-soundness cuts the headline closure-valid rates roughly in half everywhere -- benign 0.849->0.649, one-hop falsehood 0.671->0.300, global falsehood 0.393->0.213 (pooled, all-rows denominators) -- so a large share of 'valid' completions reach the target through closure-level skips rather than explicit step-by-step derivation. (2) The family gradient survives the strict standard but compresses: one-hop hop-sound-valid 0.300 (Wilson95 [0.2595, 0.3439]) vs global 0.213 (Wilson95 [0.178, 0.2535]), non-overlapping CIs, versus the permissive gap 0.671 vs 0.393. (3) Strict repair concentrates entirely at low refutation distance -- SRR 0.251 for one-hop vs 0.002 for global -- and the global-falsehood corrective conjunct is 0 partly for a structural reason: the complement of a CWA-only falsehood is itself underivable, so explicitly contradicting the plant breaks closure-validity, leaving doubt (0.013) as the only strict-repair route. (4) Unconditional injection use inverts the gradient (0.029 one-hop vs 0.378 global) and the echo/derivational split shows global plants are genuinely reused derivationally (0.351 derivational vs 0.049 echo) while one-hop negated plants show only echo (0.029, derivational n.m.), replacing the misleading 0.000-dagger cells. (5) Two caveats for interpretation: every non-benign condition omits the overwritten gold step, so hop-soundness also prices in re-deriving that step (true interruption 0.856->0.347 quantifies this structural burden with a TRUE plant), and per-row truth audit shows the legacy 'wrong' family is mostly audited-TRUE plants (355/390 entailed), so it must not be read as a falsehood family.
