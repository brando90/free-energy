## PART 1 — The professor's five weaknesses, one by one

### Weakness #1: "The causal variable is still not cleanly isolated" — addressed by Plan A (+ Plan D §5.2)

**What the plans deliver.** Plan A is a serious design: the polarity×distance attribute factorial with mirrored worlds (aff_false_dk and neg_true_dk differing by one token in the same world), the byte-identical-sentence evidence-mover (EXPE), a computable refutation-distance definition, the retro-audit of locked artifacts with usability/frequency covariates, satellites for every one of the professor's seven axes (statement type, usability, on/off-path, lexical overlap, frequency), pre-registered confirmatory tests including the outcome "professor's confound account SUPPORTED," and the design-matrix table (conditions × axes, ✓/✗/pinned). The professor's complaint was that the distance-k and certificate experiments were "not presented with enough force or detail"; Fig A/B/C plus the design matrix is exactly the requested force.

**Can I still reject on #1? Yes, on three grounds the plan creates or leaves open:**

1. **The deconfounded core cannot measure the headline phenomenon.** EXPD-CORE's planted claims are attributes, chosen *because* they are derivationally inert — but the paper's flagship claim is that far falsehoods are "silently absorbed **and reused in downstream derivation**" (abstract; Table 1 inj-dep 0.342 pooled). In every core cell, injection-dependence is mechanically impossible except as echo; the absorption DV becomes a brand-new belief probe. Derivational reuse at matched distance survives only in the satellites (`cat_false_d{1,3}_usable`, mid position only, n=150): the deconfounded *reuse* gradient rests on two distance points at one position. A reviewer writes: "the confound-free cells show a doubt gradient; the reuse gradient — the claim in your title era — was never demonstrated confound-free." **Severity: major.** *Preempt:* promote `cat_false_{d1,d2,d3,d5,unent}×{inert,usable}` to core status (2 positions, n=150), making validator-measured inj-dep available across the full distance range; keep the attribute cells as the polarity-pure companion.

2. **The belief probe conflates trace-absorption with probe-time verifiability.** The refuting rule sits in the premises at probe time; verifying "Stella is mean" when asked point-blank is itself easier at d=1 than d=5. A Δbelief gradient in d is therefore predicted even if the trace absorbed nothing — the new primary DV has the IV built into its measurement. **Severity: major (it is pre-registered as primary P2).** *Preempt:* add a trace-free probe arm (premises + planted claim asserted + question, no continuation) per cell and pre-register the DV as Δbelief(trace) − Δbelief(no-trace); the aff_true/neg_true cells already give the calibration anchors.

3. **On-chain refuting evidence collapses to distance ≈ 0 inside the scored window.** The refuting rule attaches at C_{front+d} *on Stella's goal chain*; the continuation derives C_{front+1}…C_{front+d} en route to the target, so the contradiction becomes 1-hop-visible mid-continuation in every finite-d cell. The IV is really "how many steps until the clash surfaces," not static evidence distance. The design has the fix in hand — `*_offpath_d3` — but only as a single satellite point. **Severity: moderate-major.** *Preempt:* cross offpath × {1,3,5} into the core, and pre-register a doubt-onset-timing analysis (doubt-token position relative to the step where the refuting antecedent is derived); if doubt fires only when the clash becomes local, that is itself a sharper claim — say so in PLAN2.

Smaller residual holes: mirrored worlds differ by one rule's polarity, so the double filter can select different problem cohorts across polarity cells — condition confirmatory contrasts on jointly-solved mirrored quadruples (not in the plan). EXPE-inf's j manipulation confounds inferential depth with how far ahead of the derivation front the inserted certificate jumps (j=1 = maximal goal-jump anomaly); irrelevant arms are offset-matched but not jump-depth-matched — match them, or add cert-depth as covariate. The locked data already show a d=0 < d=1 doubt non-monotonicity (contradiction doubt 0.231/0.269/0.177 vs one-hop 0.467/0.273/0.267, `paper_results.tex` lines 51–57) that the confirmatory family, which starts at d=1, silently skips — a reviewer will ask; address it explicitly.

**Adjudication: mostly neutralized for doubt; only gestured at for absorption/reuse unless the three preempts land. With them, neutralized.**

**Can I still reject on #5? Yes, on one conditional and one structural ground:**

**Adjudication: mostly neutralized — contingent on the op-free cell and matched-strictness reporting; without them, a floor outcome leaves W5 standing with a consolation narrative.**
