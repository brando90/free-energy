## 3. Canonical causal variable (single owner)

**Refutation distance** `d(a*)` = BFS depth over **direct** rule applications from the visible prefix state
to the **complement** of the planted claim `a*` — exactly `shortest_rule_distance`
(`expc_polarity_control.py:340–356`; validatorPlan Alg 5). `d=0`: complement already in state; `d=k`: k rule
applications; `d=∞`: complement underivable (falsity certified only by closed-world closure search, and
**only possible for positive categoricals** — see T3, §4).

- Fail-closed audit in every new cell: `assert measured d == designed d` (EXPB `is_fully_audited` pattern).
- Retro-computed for every locked injection (validatorPlan M5); re-bin or disclose any "global" falsehood
  found to have finite d.
- EXPG decomposes the construct: **re-read distance k_r** (trace lines back to the refuting stated value)
  × **recompute depth k_c** (unstated evaluations needed); the op-free k_c=0 row is the d≈0 analogue.
  Cross-regime point values are declared incomparable; only ordering/sign claims transfer.

## 4. Design constraints (verdict amendments — binding, enforced at generation time)

Grammar theorems, printed in the design-matrix table (they turn missing cells into propositions):

- **T1** ON-PATH × AFFIRMATIVE × FALSE is logically unconstructible (on-path positive entity facts are
  entailed-true by construction).
- **T2** Rule bodies are always positive categories (`validator.py:33–54`): attribute claims are
  derivationally inert *by grammar*. Polarity purity is tested on attributes, reuse on categoricals — by
  necessity, not choice.
- **T3** Attribute truth-decidability ⟺ finite d (attribute audit is open-world): attribute cells **cannot
  exist at d=∞**. Every ∞ anchor lives in the categorical family (`cat_false_∞`, genuinely CWA-false).

## 5. THE confirmatory family (single Holm family, m = 8, α = 0.05)

Conventions. Holm–Bonferroni over the 8 slots below; a **conjunctive slot** passes iff *every* component
rejects at that slot's Holm-adjusted α (intersection-union; no alpha splitting inside a slot). A **TOST slot**
passes iff both one-sided tests reject at the slot's Holm-adjusted α, margin ±0.10 on the rate scale.
Judge-rated doubt (§7) is the doubt DV in every slot. This is the only confirmatory family in the program;
`PLAN_PROGTRACE.md` contributes exactly one slot (H7) and nothing else.

| H | Suite | Test (pre-specified) | Passes iff |
|---|---|---|---|
| **H1** | EXPD | Negation-free doubt gradient: within `aff_false_attr` (planted sentence byte-identical across d; zero negation tokens in the stimulus), GLMM Wald test on the d slope over **d ∈ {1,2,3,5}**, doubt decreasing. | slope < 0 at Holm-α |
| **H2** | EXPD | Polarity equivalence at matched d: `aff_false_attr_d` vs `neg_false_attr_d` judge-doubt, TOST ±0.10, pooled over d ∈ {1,3} (pooling across 3 positions; stated per verdict minor-b). | TOST passes |
| **H3** | EXPD | Truth, not the "not" token (conjunction): (i) `neg_true_attr` ≈ `aff_true_attr` doubt, TOST ±0.10, pooled over d ∈ {0,1,3} at mid (n=150/d, pooled n=450); (ii) `neg_false_d1` − `neg_true_d1` > 0. Negation-cue account predicts high doubt on `neg_true`; refutability predicts low. | both components pass |
| **H4** | EXPD | Confound-free reuse gradient: **derivational** injection-dependence (M4 split, per MAJOR-2) over d ∈ {1,3,∞} within `cat_false_usable`, d coded as an ordered factor, linear trend contrast, reuse increasing as counterevidence recedes. | trend > 0 at Holm-α |

### 5.1 Pre-stated outcome branches

- **Local refutability CONFIRMED** iff H1, H4, H5 pass and H2, H3 pass: the gradient exists with zero
  negation tokens, reuse tracks d in usable categoricals, and derivability — not lexical co-presence —
  moves doubt. H6 strengthens (within-problem); H7 extends the claim to a second, better-instrumented
  regime; H8 adds the mediator (initiation) leg.
- **Professor's confound account SUPPORTED** — a publishable, pre-registered outcome, and it will be
  reported as such — iff any of: (i) H2 fails with neg ≫ aff **and** H1 flat (negation is the driver);
  (ii) H5 fails with FREQ ≈ REFUTING (lexical priming is the driver); (iii) EXPD gradients vanish while
  the legacy negated doubt gradients reproduce (the legacy curve was a negation/parse artifact — Stage 0
  has already adjudicated the *validity* half of this in favor of artifact; the doubt half is what remains
  live).
- **Partial — flagging without reuse:** doubt gradient (H1) without a reuse gradient (H4) → claims
  re-centered on flagging + the absorption boundary; the title-era absorption claim is dropped from the
  headline (pre-authorized re-centering).
- **Partial — EXPG floor branch (gate G-B, scope-bounding):** if Stage-1 pilot shows neither
  judge-doubt ≥ 0.10 nor absorption ≤ 0.85 on adjacent/op-free k_r=1 cells, run only the compact
  floor-documentation package and report the pre-registered boundary result ("the model fails to re-check
  even when checking is a zero-compute re-read of a visible line"); the generality claim retreats to
  "protocol and taxonomy transfer; the evidence-distance gradient is conditional on task check-affordance,"
  stated in §5.3/Limitations, out of the abstract.
- **Partial — EXPG channel splits** (pre-committed decision tree, `PLAN_PROGTRACE.md`): gradient in
  absorption but not doubt (or converse) → split the claim by channel; k_r gradient without k_c →
  construct renamed **re-readable-evidence availability** (confirms main.tex §3.4 causally); k_c without
  k_r → verification-compute framing; gradient only under redundancy/pre-late positions → moderator scope
  language. In every branch: all cells reported, H7 adjudicated exactly as registered.
- **EXPF branches:** H8 passes with small true-cost → initiation-mediator claim promoted; H8 passes with
  large true-cost → same claim with the cost printed in the same sentence; H8 fails → mediator claim
  dropped, EXPF reported in appendix, doubt-forcing pilot language deleted from main text.
- **EXPC_FULL (completed):** already adjudicated descriptively (§2.3); no confirmatory scoring.

## 9. Analysis and reporting rules

1. **Model spec (EXPD primary):** mixed-effects logistic per DV —
   `outcome ~ d + polarity + d:polarity + position + covariates + (1 | world_family)`,
   `world_family` = mirrored-quadruple/base-chain id (one instance per world ⇒ **quadruple is the cluster
   unit**). Implementation: `src/mixed_models.py` (pymer4/lme4 or statsmodels; dependency pinned).
2. **Cohort conditioning (EXPD):** confirmatory contrasts on jointly-solved mirrored quadruples **and**
   identical gold prefixes up to si (MOD-13); per-world-version solve rates and prefix-divergence rate
   reported; robustness fit with divergence as covariate.
3. **EXPE:** McNemar within problem cluster (problems are paired across arms by construction).
   **EXPG:** `wilson` + `cluster_boot_rate` (program = cluster), verbatim from
   `expa_global_expansion.py:738–767` or a factored `stats_common.py`; Holm within EXPG only for its
   secondary family — its confirmatory contribution is H7 alone.
4. **Covariates** (planted-sentence token length, template class, in-question planted-token count
   (constant 1 by design), parse-rate cell means, injection sent_idx) are logged everywhere, used in
   robustness fits only, never in headline models.
5. **Legacy reconciliation:** locked tables keep the cluster-bootstrap logistic; one appendix paragraph
   reconciles the two frameworks. Locked result dirs are never re-run; promoted legacy summaries get
