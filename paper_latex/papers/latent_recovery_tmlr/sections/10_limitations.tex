\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

% source: PAPER_SKELETON Sec 10; reframed 10.1 to non-replication finding.

\paragraph{The controlled base is a single model family, and we now know the
boundary conditions are model-specific.} Every controlled verdict is on
Qwen2.5-7B. The multi-model replication of Section~\ref{sec:usability} turns
this from an open caveat into a finding: the visibility cliff and the
flatness framing do not generalize, and the usability asymmetry, while the
most portable, is scale-bounded. Readers should treat the anchor-model laws
as scoped to that model unless and until further replication extends them.
% source: E1_ADJUDICATION Sec 4; PAPER_SKELETON 10.1

\paragraph{The domain is synthetic logic, and the second regime is only
piloted.} The worlds are PrOntoQA-style first-order-logic problems, chosen
for formal checkability, and the program-trace recompute regime is a pilot
rather than a full experiment. A broader program-execution run would widen
the recompute-universality claim beyond the models on those cells; we treat
that run as optional for this venue and mark the scope accordingly.
% source: PAPER_SKELETON 10.2; \pending{E2 EXPG-FULL optional}

\paragraph{The validator and judge carry caveats, and human validation is
pending.} The strict hop-sound standard prices in re-deriving the omitted
gold step, which the true-plant control bounds; the global cell's near-zero
strict-repair is partly structural, because the complement of a
closed-world-only falsehood is itself underivable, so an explicit correction
breaks closure validity; and verbalized doubt is a demoted lexical proxy
reported only as a contrast.
% source: PAPER_SKELETON 10.3; EVIDENCE_protocol_prereg P4 caveats 1-2
The planned human-blinded rater audit has not been run, so the
strict-repair credibility clause is held pending. \tododv{E4 human $\kappa$
audit gates the SRR credibility claim.}
% source: PAPER_SKELETON 10.3 needs-human-validation

\paragraph{Perturbed problems over-represent long chains, and the generated
worlds are easier than the natural cohort.} Proof compression filters short
chains out, so the perturbed set skews long, and the generated
controlled worlds are easier than the natural cohort, with a manipulation
anchor that does not pass its own equivalence gate by a small margin. The
natural usable-versus-inert comparison is position- and length-unmatched and
uses a small cell, so it is qualitative.
% source: PAPER_SKELETON 10.4; EVIDENCE_lineage_boundaries L-lab.2 (funnels); F-use.4 caveat

\paragraph{One error type is out of scope.} The single most frequent natural
error object is a fabricated or mis-spliced rule, not a false entity-fact,
and no planted family emulates it except in inert form, so our claims are
scoped to entity-fact falsehoods and the rule case is a stated gap.
% source: PAPER_SKELETON 10.5; EVIDENCE_protocol_prereg P7 caveat 1
