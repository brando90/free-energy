\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

% source: PAPER_SKELETON Sec 11.

\paragraph{A boundary-condition map, honestly scoped.} Under an audited
protocol, in-stream verification of a planted falsehood on one well-studied
model is governed by visibility and usability rather than by any distance
gradient. That map does not generalize: the visibility cliff is specific to
Qwen2.5-7B and reverses elsewhere, and usable-error absorption collapses with
scale. Two things survive. No model we tested re-verifies a value it must
recompute, and verbal rejection is a weak proxy that a derivation-licensed
split is needed to read. We offer the whole as a map of where in-stream
self-checking happens, with an explicit account of how far each boundary
travels, and we take the non-replication itself to be the most useful thing
the audit produced.
