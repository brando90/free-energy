\paragraph{Correction structure.} The anchor family used a Holm correction
over its six tests; equivalence claims (polarity, flatness) use two
one-sided tests; the multi-model replication uses family-cluster bootstrap
confidence intervals; the question-flip primary uses a paired cluster
bootstrap against a minimum effect size of 0.05.

\paragraph{Amendment and deviation log.} Multi-model replication: no material
deviations; OLMo-2-7B is reported but not scored (greedy gold-solve 0.199).
Question-flip experiment: registration amendment \texttt{d367a97} folded the
crossed verification factor into the confirmatory family and set the
candidate pool to 750; implementation fix \texttt{f447bc5} extended an
exhausted nonce-noun pool during CPU world construction before any outcome
data existed (adjudicated benign, first 9{,}716 yields byte-identical); the
confirmatory queue omitted the ITT sensitivity invocation, which the
adjudicator ran separately (sign agrees, both fail the MES bar); the power
curve is degenerate and plays no role in the verdict.

\paragraph{Confirmatory-versus-exploratory accounting.} Fixed in advance and
carrying full weight: the polarity control (the one surviving anchor test),
and the three replication conditions with their thresholds. Fixed in advance
but direction-open (motivating, not clinching): the $d{=}0$ visibility
contrast, and the frequency-matched lexical arm. Exploratory, reported as
suggestive only: the reuse-highest-at-one-hop reversal, and the cheap-one-hop
checking spike, whose matched follow-up (the question-flip experiment) returns
a null. Held pending external validation: the
strict-repair credibility claim (pending the E4 human $\kappa$ audit), and
the question-flip null (branch~(b), \emph{provisional} pending the blind
non-author classifier-transfer audit; if per-arm classifier error differs by

\section{Legacy gap decomposition (reviewer Q1)}
\label{app:legacy}

% source: EVIDENCE_findings_core F-use.5; EVIDENCE_protocol_prereg P3.

The legacy one-hop-versus-global gap is not an artifact of $d{=}0$
mis-binning. Under identical measurement, excluding the 14.9 percent of
rows that measure at distance zero keeps or widens every gap, because those
rows are low-doubt and low-closure. The closure-valid gap moves from
$+0.278$ to $+0.299$ once $d{=}0$ rows are excluded, and the strict
hop-sound gap from $+0.087$ to $+0.095$.
% source: EVIDENCE_findings_core F-use.5 (table, 14/14 anchors exact)
Identical measurement is not the same as matched claims: this removes
distance mis-binning, not the polarity and lexical confound, which is
answered by the polarity deconfound (Section~\ref{sec:reversal}) and the
frequency-matched arm (Section~\ref{sec:instrument}) instead.
% source: EVIDENCE_findings_core F-use.5 caveats

\section{The certificate experiment (reviewer W3)}
\label{app:expb}

Adding a directly-contradicting local certificate does not cleanly convert
injection-dependent continuations into clean ones; it redistributes the
whole continuation-style distribution into unparsed, from 4.0 percent to
31.7 percent. Under honest worst-case accounting of the unparsed rows, only
the verbalized-doubt contrast survives, at a net $+0.37$ with a McNemar
$p{=}7.70\times10^{-34}$ and a bootstrap CI of $[0.31, 0.43]$.
% source: EVIDENCE_lineage_boundaries L-expb.1 (unparsed 0.040->0.317; doubt +0.3700 p=7.70e-34)
The 32B judge then reads 86 of 95 of those unparsed rows as explicit
rejection, which both tightens the worst-case bound to about $-0.10$ and
re-attributes the effect to $d{=}0$ visibility, because the certificate
states the complement in the prefix. The certificate experiment therefore
corroborates the visibility cliff rather than a distinct
counterevidence-accessibility claim.
% source: EVIDENCE_lineage_boundaries L-expb.2 (86/95 reject; sign fixed -0.10)

\section{Strict metrics}
\label{app:strict}

The strict hop-sound standard roughly halves closure-valid completion
everywhere and surfaces structure that the permissive standard hides. On
the false-attribute cells, closure-valid rates of 0.90 to 0.96 fall to 0.07
to 0.25 hop-sound, so only 11 to 27 percent of closure-valid completions are
genuine step-by-step derivations. The strict pass covers 6{,}147 rows,
verified three independent ways.
