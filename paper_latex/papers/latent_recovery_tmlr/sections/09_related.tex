\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

% source: RELATED_WORK_MAP P1-P5; concessions Sec 2; must-adds Sec 3.

\paragraph{Perturbing a model's own reasoning stream is heavily
precedented; our novelty is the audited package.} Injecting or perturbing a
model's chain of thought and watching what follows is an established design
\citep{vonrecum2026robust, aravindan2026fragile, shah2025reflection,
amjith2025flawed, han2026rfeval}. The closest predecessors intervene on
their own traces and report downstream accuracy or a doubt expression
\citep{vonrecum2026robust}, or measure faithfulness as stance consistency
under output-level interventions \citep{han2026rfeval}. We do not claim the
inject-and-continue skeleton. What we add is the five-part audited package:
a per-plant truth audit, a four-way formal validator, a measured rather than
designed refutation distance, a derivation-licensed split, and a
usability manipulation. Our lexically-cued-doubt result also cautions
against reading a doubt-mediated ``recovery'' as genuine checking
\citep{vonrecum2026robust}.
% source: RELATED_WORK_MAP P1

\paragraph{Self-correction fails on a model's own output, and we
corroborate rather than discover that boundary.} The self-correction
literature establishes that models correct external errors better than their
own \citep{huang2024cannot, tyen2024mistakes, kamoi2024selfcorrection}. Most
directly, Self-Correction Bench names and quantifies the blind spot first,
across many models with a controlled injection design
\citep{tsui2025blindspot}, and a chat-template-role account reproduces the
same own-versus-external asymmetry \citep{chen2026selfcorrection}. We concede
priority on the boundary itself. Our production-boundary result corroborates
and extends this to formally audited logic, adding a silent-reuse measure
their answer-naming judges miss and a near-total magnitude. The prefill
regime we probe is the in-stream continuation that autoregressive
consistency sustains and that vanishes across a turn boundary
\citep{qi2025shallow, lyu2026autoregressive, park2026inference}.
% source: RELATED_WORK_MAP P2; concession 1

\paragraph{Detection-without-action is known; our addition is the measured
distance axis and the usability axis.} Internal probes can detect a model's
own errors while behavior does not act on them, a diagnostic-not-causal
dissociation \citep{yuan2026hidden, yee2024dissociation}, with internal
self-repair and correctness machinery documented separately
\citep{mcgrath2023hydra, lad2024stages, sun2026trajectories}. Process and
step verifiers exist as external modules \citep{lightman2024verify,
zheng2025processbench, you2025probabilistic}; our question is instead whether
the generator checks in-stream and unprompted. The nearest neighbor to our
usability axis silently adopts simple hints while verbalizing complex ones
\citep{chen2025reasoning}\pending{home the simple-adopted / complex-verbalized
dissociation on Unspoken Hints 2509.26041 once abs-verified; chen2025reasoning
otherwise carries only the ``under 20 percent verbalized'' claim in P4}; we
instead manipulate graded derivational
usability against a formal validator and measure silent downstream reuse,
which we mark as the cleanest novel result and lead with its flatness across
distance. Question-side premise-critique and conflicting-knowledge work
\citep{li2025pcbench, feng2026track, feng2025misinformation} contrasts with
our trace-side plant.
% source: RELATED_WORK_MAP P3

\paragraph{Verbalized reasoning can be unfaithful, which grounds our lexical
caution.} Chain-of-thought text can misrepresent the true driver of an
answer \citep{turpin2023unfaithful, lanham2023measuring, arcuschin2026wild},
with under 20 percent of decisive hints verbalized \citep{chen2025reasoning},
and invalid chains retain most of their gains \citep{schaeffer2023invalid,
wang2023understanding}, which is why doubt-shaped text is not a check
\citep{kambhampati2026position}. Against this lineage our contribution is a
behavioral fabricated-rule signature specific to rejection text: essentially
all control rejections invent a nonexistent rule, versus roughly 80 percent
derivation-backed for genuine evidence, a citable caution that discredits
face-value doubt classifiers\pending{contrast against the prior probe-based
rationalization-detection operationalization Catching Rationalization
2603.17199 once abs-verified}.

\paragraph{Computation-versus-validation, training recipe, and a held-out
motivated-reasoning direction.} A mechanistic analysis shows models compute
arithmetic but fail to validate it, via surface consistency heads
\citep{bertolazzi2025validation}; we concede mechanistic priority and offer
our universal recompute boundary as the behavioral corroboration\pending{add
Trust-but-Don't-Verify 2606.05403 here as the behavioral corroborator of the
recompute boundary once abs-verified}. Our
non-monotone cross-lineage pattern, where a mid-tier frontier model absorbs
worse than the open anchor and the oldest model is deepest, complicates
clean training-recipe accounts of the blind spot and the reflection-emerges-
from-RL story \citep{guo2025deepseek, yang2025aha, daliberti2026illusion}\pending{the
clean SFT(bad) / RL(good) spectral-radius account SPARC 2607.09803, which this
non-monotone lineage complicates, TODO-verify by ~2026-08-15}.
Finally, the usefulness of a planted statement does not detectably change
genuine checking. A tempting account holds that checking is suppressed when
the planted error is useful to the model's current goal; its nearest analogue
gates suppression on task compliance rather than derivational usefulness
\citep{tsui2025blindspot}\pending{cite must-add stubs Knowing-but-Not-
Correcting 2605.05957, Trust-but-Don't-Verify 2606.05403, and choice-
supportive-bias 2507.03120 once abs-verified}. A matched-pair construction
that pits a useful against a useless version of the same planted statement on
clean worlds (the question-flip experiment) shows no such effect: the checking
gap is null (gap $+0.004$, 95\% CI $[-0.002, +0.010]$), and the earlier
exploratory one-hop contrast that suggested goal-sensitivity does not
replicate under this matched construction.
